Heritage

Born in Florence,
Refined by Time.

Our journey began in a small atelier tucked away in the shadows of the Duomo. It was here that our founder, Alessandro Rossi, sought to marry the rugged durability of Tuscan leather with the ethereal softness of silk.

Today, Elégance remains a family-owned house, preserving the same hand-stitching techniques that made our first valise a masterpiece. Every piece we create is a bridge between the golden age of Italian tailoring and the minimalist future.
